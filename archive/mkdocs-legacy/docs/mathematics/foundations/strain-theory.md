# Strain theory

CALM interface matching and construction use strain quantities to compare and transform in-plane cells. Specification pages should identify the strain measure, deformation gradient, principal quantities, units, and admissibility thresholds.

Initial topics to expand:

- deformation gradients;
- principal stretches and logarithmic/Hencky strain;
- strain partitioning between two slabs;
- objective strain metrics used for candidate ranking and filtering.
