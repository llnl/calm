# Notation

This page reserves shared notation for CALM mathematical specifications.

## Objects and spaces

- Crystals, surfaces, slabs, interface candidates, built interface structures, evaluation results, datasets, and reports are scientific objects.
- Lattice, Cartesian, fractional-coordinate, reciprocal, surface, slab, interface, transformation, search, evaluation, and persistence spaces are mathematical or representation spaces.

## Matrices and vectors

Unless a page states otherwise, lattice basis vectors are represented as columns of a matrix. A cell matrix therefore maps fractional coordinates to Cartesian coordinates by left multiplication.

## Tolerances

Tolerances should be named, dimensionality should be explicit, and unit-bearing quantities should state their units.
