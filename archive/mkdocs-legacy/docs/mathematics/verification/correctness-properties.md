# Correctness properties

Correctness properties should be stated at the mathematical level before being translated into tests.

Initial categories:

- identity preservation under representation morphisms;
- equivalence under admissible basis changes;
- invariance of persisted scientific identifiers where compatibility requires it;
- monotonicity or admissibility properties of filters;
- reproducibility of deterministic algorithms;
- bounded variability of stochastic algorithms under documented settings.
