\chapter{M005 --- Slab Specification}

\section{Stage 2 --- Mathematical Analysis}

\subsection{Objective}

Establish the mathematical properties of the Slab Specification object
independently of its geometric realization.

\subsection{Definition}

A Slab Specification is the complete set of scientific parameters
required to realize a finite slab from a selected Surface Termination.

\subsection{Proposition M005.1}

For every admissible Surface Termination and admissible specification
parameters there exists a unique Slab Specification.

\subsection{Proof Strategy}

Show that specification parameters are independent of the later
geometric realization and uniquely determine the intended slab model.

\subsection{Open Questions}

\begin{itemize}
\item Minimum admissible thickness.
\item Vacuum semantics.
\item Symmetric versus asymmetric specifications.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Completion Criterion}

Stage 2 completes when the Slab Specification is fully characterized
without reference to implementation geometry.
