\chapter{M003 --- Termination Enumeration}

\section{Stage 1 --- Revised Mathematical Specification}

\subsection{Reason for Revision}

Inspection of the implementation shows that the immediate scientific
transformation is the enumeration of admissible surface terminations,
not the selection of a particular termination.

\subsection{Scientific Purpose}

Construct the finite set of admissible surface terminations associated
with a primitive surface.

\subsection{Transformation}

\[
\Phi_{\mathrm{enumerate}}:
\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{primitive}}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{T})
\]

where $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{T})$ denotes the finite set of admissible
surface terminations.

\subsection{Input}

Primitive Surface.

\subsection{Output}

Termination Set.

\subsection{Invariants}

\begin{itemize}
\item In-plane lattice.
\item Surface orientation.
\item Bulk crystallographic identity.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Notes}

Selection of a particular termination is deferred to the next
transformation (M004).
