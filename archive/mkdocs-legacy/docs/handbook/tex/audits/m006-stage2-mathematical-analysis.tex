\chapter{M006 --- Finite Slab Geometry}

\section{Stage 2 --- Mathematical Analysis}

\subsection{Objective}

Establish the mathematical properties of realizing a finite slab geometry
from a Slab Specification.

\subsection{Definition}

A Finite Slab Geometry is an explicit finite atomic realization satisfying
a Slab Specification while preserving crystallographic invariants.

\subsection{Proposition M006.1}

Every admissible Slab Specification determines at least one finite slab
geometry satisfying its specification.

\subsection{Proof Strategy}

Show:
\begin{enumerate}
\item specification parameters define a finite realization domain;
\item atomic coordinates can be constructed consistently;
\item boundary conditions are satisfied;
\item realization preserves specification invariants.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Open Questions}

\begin{itemize}
\item Uniqueness of realization.
\item Rigid-body gauge freedoms.
\item Treatment of vacuum regions.
\end{itemize}
