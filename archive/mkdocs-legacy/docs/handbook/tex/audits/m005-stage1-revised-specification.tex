\chapter{M005 --- Slab Specification}

\section{Stage 1 --- Revised Mathematical Specification}

\subsection{Reason for Revision}

Inspection of the implementation indicates that slab construction is more
naturally decomposed into specification, geometric realization, and
final slab representation.

\subsection{Scientific Purpose}

Construct a Slab Specification from a selected Surface Termination.

\subsection{Transformation}

\[
\Phi_{\mathrm{spec}}:
\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{surface}}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{spec}}
\]

\subsection{Inputs}

\begin{itemize}
\item Surface Termination
\item Thickness parameters
\item Boundary-condition parameters
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Output}

A Slab Specification.

\subsection{Scientific Content}

A Slab Specification records the scientific parameters required to
realize a slab without yet constructing its finite geometry.

\subsection{Invariants}

\begin{itemize}
\item Selected surface termination
\item Crystallographic orientation
\item In-plane lattice
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Deferred Transformations}

Finite geometric realization and canonical slab construction are treated
as subsequent transformations pending implementation review.
