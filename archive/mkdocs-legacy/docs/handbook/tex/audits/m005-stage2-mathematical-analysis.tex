\chapter{M005 --- Slab Construction}

\section{Stage 2 --- Mathematical Analysis}

\subsection{Objective}

Establish the mathematical properties of slab construction
independently of the implementation.

\subsection{Definition}

A slab is a finite geometric realization of a selected surface
termination with explicit thickness and boundary-condition parameters.

\subsection{Proposition M005.1}

Given an admissible surface termination and valid slab parameters,
there exists a corresponding slab.

\subsection{Proof Strategy}

Show:
\begin{enumerate}
\item finite thickness determines a finite realization;
\item boundary conditions preserve the selected termination;
\item stacking is well-defined;
\item gauge-equivalent realizations represent the same slab.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Open Questions}

\begin{itemize}
\item Vacuum vs. non-vacuum formulations.
\item Symmetric vs. asymmetric slabs.
\item Minimum admissible thickness.
\end{itemize}
