
\chapter{M001 --- Crystal $\rightarrow$ Surface Frame}

\section{Stage 1 --- Mathematical Specification}

This stage supersedes the legacy transformation document and serves as the
canonical entry point for the M001 audit.

\subsection{Scientific Transformation}

\[
\mathrm{Crystal} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Surface\ Frame}
\]

\subsection{Purpose}

Construct the unique surface frame associated with a selected crystallographic
surface.

\subsection{Scientific Input}

Crystal.

\subsection{Scientific Output}

Surface Frame.

\subsection{Preconditions}

A valid crystal and a selected surface orientation.

\subsection{Postconditions}

A surface frame consistent with the selected orientation.

\subsection{Invariants}

Crystallographic orientation, lattice consistency, handedness, and scientific
identity of the crystal are preserved.

\subsection{Gauge Freedoms}

Equivalent basis choices and origin conventions that preserve the same surface
frame.
