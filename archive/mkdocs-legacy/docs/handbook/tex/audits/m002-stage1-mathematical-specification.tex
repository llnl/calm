
\chapter{M002 --- Primitive Surface Construction}
\label{chap:m002}

\section{Stage 1 --- Mathematical Specification}

\subsection{Scientific Purpose}

Construct the primitive two-dimensional periodic surface associated with
a previously constructed Surface Frame.

\subsection{Transformation}

\[
\Phi_{\mathrm{surface}} :
\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{surface}}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{primitive}}
\]

where a Surface Frame is the adapted lattice frame produced by M001 and
a Primitive Surface is the minimal periodic two-dimensional crystal
embedded in that frame.

\subsection{Mathematical Inputs}

\begin{itemize}
\item Surface Frame
\item Crystal motif
\item Oriented lattice basis
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Mathematical Outputs}

\begin{itemize}
\item Primitive surface lattice
\item Primitive surface motif
\item Surface orientation
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Preconditions}

\begin{itemize}
\item Valid Surface Frame.
\item Primitive Miller orientation.
\item Consistent crystallographic basis.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Postconditions}

\begin{itemize}
\item Primitive periodic surface.
\item Preserved crystallographic orientation.
\item Preserved lattice equivalence.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Invariants}

\begin{itemize}
\item Surface normal.
\item Chemical identities.
\item Two-dimensional periodicity.
\item Crystallographic equivalence.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Gauge Freedom}

Equivalent primitive representatives, in-plane origin shifts and basis
choices that preserve the represented primitive surface.
